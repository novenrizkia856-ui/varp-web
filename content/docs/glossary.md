# Glossary

## Asset
The tokenized financial instrument being described by Varp.

## Underlying Asset
The stock or other real world asset referenced by the tokenized asset.

## Rights Profile
The normalized machine readable representation of ownership, economic, governance, transfer, redemption, DeFi, lifecycle, and verification information.

## Evidence
Information used to support a Rights Profile field.

## Onchain Evidence
Evidence observed directly from blockchain contracts, state, events, roles, permissions, and behavior.

## Offchain Evidence
Evidence from issuer, custodian, legal, offering, eligibility, redemption, or corporate action documents.

## Rights Engine
The system that converts raw evidence into Varp's normalized rights schema.

## Verification Engine
The system that determines whether evidence is complete and internally consistent.

## Rights Diff
A structured comparison of the rights and restrictions of two or more tokenized assets.

## Permissioned Transfer
A transfer model where movement of the token depends on approval, identity, allowlists, jurisdiction, eligibility, or other conditions.

## Whitelist / Allowlist
A list or rule set that determines which addresses are allowed to hold, receive, or interact with an asset.

## Redemption
The process through which a holder can exchange or convert the token according to the asset's documented terms.

## Corporate Action
An issuer related event such as a vote, distribution, split, merger, redemption, or other action affecting holder rights.

## Conflict
A Varp verification state used when important evidence sources disagree.

## Profile Anchor
A compact onchain record containing a profile hash, version, status, or metadata reference for a Rights Profile.
